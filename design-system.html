✦  Brand & interface guidelines · v1.0 · 2026

The design system

One source of truth for La Raza's digital presence, built from the logo identity, the Motion reveal and the film of the fit-out. Gold on night. Sculptural, unhurried, generous.

01

Gold on night

Black is the room; gold is the light in it. Never reverse to gold backgrounds for large areas. Gold is precious, so use it for meaning.

02

One centre

Like the emblem, compositions gather around a centre: centred heroes, symmetrical lockups, the arch as a doorway.

03

Unhurried motion

Everything eases in slowly, the way light comes up in a room. Lines draw, then fill. Nothing bounces, nothing flashes.

01 Logo

The emblem & wordmark

An abstract butterfly: elegant, sculptural forms that come together around one centre. It stands for individuality, connection and transformation. The script wordmark is part of the logo and is never typeset.

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space equal to half the emblem's width on every side. Minimum sizes: emblem 20px / 6mm; horizontal lockup 96px / 25mm; wordmark alone 72px.

Don't

  • Stretch or distort
  • Recolour off-palette
  • Rotate
  • Place on busy, bright imagery

04 Space & shape

Room to breathe

A 4px base, generous section rhythm (--section-y 96 → 224px) and three shapes: the hairline, the pill and the arch.

05 Motion

Draw, then fill

Taken from the La Raza Motion film: a fine gold line draws the emblem, the form fills with gold, and the wordmark resolves out of a soft blur. Every reveal on the site follows that grammar.

07 Pattern & illustration

The lattice

The emblem repeats on a 45° hairline lattice, as on the identity's packaging. Use it behind calls to action, always under a dark radial scrim so content stays legible.

Line illustrations

Single-weight 1.5px gold line, round caps, drawn in on reveal like the Motion emblem. Subjects come from the real room: rings of light, the cloche, the coupe.

08 Film & imagery

The grade

All footage is graded toward the identity: crushed warm blacks, highlights pushed to champagne gold, saturation pulled down, a soft vignette. Cut slow (≈2× slow-motion, 0.6s dissolves) and favour light and texture over faces and logos.

FFmpeg recipe

hue=s=0.28,
eq=contrast=1.12:gamma=0.9,
curves=r='0/0 0.3/0.13 0.65/0.6 1/0.99'
      :g='0/0 0.3/0.095 0.65/0.48 1/0.9'
      :b='0/0 0.3/0.06 0.65/0.33 1/0.73',
vignette=PI/4
  • Do: chandeliers, glass, texture, timber, hands at work, warm light.
  • Don't: contractor branding, phone numbers, hi-vis close-ups, unflattering faces.
  • Delivery: hero 1280×720 H.264 (≈1.5MB, 10s loop), portrait 720×1080 for mobile; always muted, looping, with a poster.

09 Voice

Confident. Warm. Close.

We build anticipation without shouting. Short declarations in display, warmth in the serif, clarity in the sans. Social posts can carry emoji; the website uses the ✦ star instead.

Say

"Something extraordinary is on its way."

"The food. The atmosphere. The experience."

"Be first through the door."

Avoid

"BEST RESTAURANT IN TOWN!!! 🔥🔥"

Discounts, urgency tricks, slang that dates.
